# Summit Gear Co. — Employee Handbook (2026)

*Internal document. Summit Gear Co. is a fictional company created for this lab.*

## About Summit Gear Co.

Summit Gear Co. designs and sells outdoor equipment for hikers, climbers, and
backcountry travelers. The company was founded in 2011 and is headquartered in
Boulder, Colorado, with a second office in Portland, Oregon.

## Offices

Summit Gear Co. operates **two offices**:

- **Boulder, Colorado (Headquarters)** — product, engineering, and leadership.
- **Portland, Oregon** — design, marketing, and customer support.

The Boulder headquarters is **dog-friendly**. For health and safety reasons, the
Portland office does not permit pets on-site.

## Working Hours

Summit Gear operates on **core hours of 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM Mountain Time**.
Employees are expected to be available during core hours and may arrange the rest
of their schedule with their manager.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely **up to 2 days per week**. Remote days must be agreed
in advance with your manager and logged in the team calendar.

> Note: Remote-work rules are reviewed periodically. Always check for the most
> recent People Team memo, as updated guidance supersedes this handbook.

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

Full-time employees receive **22 days of paid time off per year**, plus their
**birthday off** as an additional paid day. PTO begins accruing on the first day
of employment and can be taken once approved by your manager.

Unused PTO of up to 5 days may be carried into the following calendar year.

## Equipment Allowance

Every employee receives an annual **$500 gear allowance** to spend on Summit Gear
products, and a **40% employee discount** on all other purchases.

## Expenses

Business expenses under $75 do not require pre-approval. Expenses of $75 or more
must be approved by your manager before purchase. All expenses must be submitted
within 30 days with a receipt.

## Code of Conduct

Summit Gear expects all employees to treat colleagues, customers, and partners
with respect. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated. Concerns can be raised
with any manager or with the People Team.
