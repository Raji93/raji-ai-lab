## Summit X200 Headlamp

The Summit X200 is our flagship rechargeable headlamp, built for multi-day
backcountry trips.

| Specification        | Value                          |
|----------------------|--------------------------------|
| Max brightness       | 400 lumens                     |
| Battery life (low)   | 47 hours                       |
| Battery life (high)  | 6 hours                        |
| Weight               | 92 grams                       |
| Water resistance     | IPX7 (submersible to 1 meter)  |
| Charging             | USB-C, full charge in 2.5 hours|
| Price                | $89                            |

The X200 ships with a red night-vision mode and a lock function to prevent
accidental activation in a pack.

## Trailblazer 45L Backpack

A lightweight multi-day pack designed for 2–4 night trips.

| Specification        | Value                          |
|----------------------|--------------------------------|
| Capacity             | 45 liters                      |
| Weight               | 1.4 kilograms                  |
| Frame                | Internal aluminum frame        |
| Hydration compatible | Yes (up to 3L reservoir)       |
| Rain cover included  | Yes                            |
| Price                | $210                           |

The Trailblazer 45L is available in three colors: Slate, Moss, and Ember.

## Warranty

All Summit Gear hardware carries a **3-year limited warranty** covering
manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover normal wear, misuse, or damage
from unauthorized repairs.
