# People Team Memo — Updated Remote Work Policy

*Internal document. Summit Gear Co. is a fictional company created for this lab.*

**Date:** March 2026
**From:** Summit Gear People Team
**Subject:** Update to remote work guidance

Effective **March 2026**, employees may now work remotely **up to 3 days per week**,
an increase from the previous limit of 2 days.

This change applies to all full-time employees in both the Boulder and Portland
offices. Remote days should still be agreed in advance with your manager and
logged in the team calendar.

All other elements of the remote-work policy in the Employee Handbook remain
unchanged. This memo supersedes the remote-work section of the 2026 Employee
Handbook where the two differ.
