Just two things to get in place:

---

# 💻 1. Bring a Laptop

A **laptop is strongly recommended**.

The Microsoft Foundry portal can be difficult to use on phones and tablets, so for the best experience, use:

- ✅ Microsoft Edge
- ✅ Google Chrome
- ✅ Mozilla Firefox

You'll also want a reliable Wi-Fi connection.

> 💡 If you're attending virtually, keep this guide open in one browser tab and Microsoft Foundry open in another.

---

# ☁️ 2. Create a Free Azure Account

You'll access Microsoft Foundry through:👉 **[ai.azure.com](https://ai.azure.com)**

To get started, create a free Azure account:

🎁 **https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/pricing/purchase-options/azure-account?icid=azurefreeaccount**

### What you'll get

✅ **$200 Azure credit** (valid for 30 days)

✅ Free monthly usage of 20+ Azure services

✅ More than enough resources for this workshop

### During signup

You'll need:

- A **personal Microsoft account** or **GitHub account**
- A **phone number** for verification
- A **non-prepaid credit or debit card** for identity verification

### Important

✅ **You will not be charged**

Azure free accounts include spending protection, and any temporary authorization charge is automatically reversed.

⏱️ Plan for approximately **15 minutes** to complete the setup.

---

> ⚠️ **Please use a personal Azure account, not a work or corporate account.**
>
> Many company-managed tenants restrict resource creation, which can block steps in this workshop.
>
> A fresh personal Azure account avoids permission issues and ensures everyone can follow along successfully.
>
> The free account offer is available to **new Azure customers** and is limited to **one account per person**.
