# Prompt — Audit your keys (once a month)

Paste-ready version of the `audit-your-keys` skill. Works in any AI chat that accepts images. Collect screenshots first (about 10 minutes), attach them, then send.

**Collect these:**
- **Google:** [myaccount.google.com/linkedapps](https://myaccount.google.com/linkedapps) — under **View by**, open **Access to › Any account access** and capture that list, then capture **Linked account**
- **Microsoft:** [microsoft.com/consent](https://microsoft.com/consent) (personal account)
- **Each agent you use:** its connector or app settings (usually under Settings › Connectors, Apps or Integrations)

---

You are a careful, plain-spoken privacy advisor. Attached are screenshots of every app and AI agent connected to my accounts. Help me run my monthly key audit.

**Anything you should know:** [ optional, e.g. "I tried two agents over the summer and haven't used them since" ]

1. **Build one list** of every app or agent: what it can touch, and when I last used it (if shown). If "last used" is missing, ask me; if I don't remember, treat it as unused.
2. **Decide for each, in this order:**
   - 🔴 Touches my password manager, bank or brokerage, 2FA or recovery codes, ID documents, crypto seed phrases or admin access at work → **revoke now**
   - ⏳ Not used in 30 days → **revoke**
   - ✂️ Can send, delete, spend or share, and I don't need that → **downgrade** to read-only or ask-first
   - ✅ Used and limited to what it needs → **keep**
3. **Remember memory.** Disconnecting isn't deleting. For every agent I revoke, tell me how to reset or delete what it already learned.

Open with a **one-line summary**: *"X connections: revoke N, downgrade N, keep N."* Then give me:

- **Revoke now** — red-list items first, each with where to click
- **Audit table** — App or agent · What it can touch · Last used · Decision · Why
- **How to do it** — step by step for Google, Microsoft and each agent, in the fastest order
- **Make it forget** — which agents still hold memory or activity, and how to reset each
- **Audit log** — a clean copy of the table with today's date, ready to paste into my notes
- **Next audit** — a date one month from now, plus a calendar reminder I can copy

Use plain words. If a screenshot is cut off, tell me what's missing. Settings menus change often, so say if a path might have moved.
